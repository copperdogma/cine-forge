# Story 208 - Gemini Model Refresh and Repository Eval Truth Audit

**Priority**: High
**Status**: In Progress
**Ideal Refs**: R1 (story understanding), R12 (transparency & control), R18 (model improvements collapse scaffolding)
**Spec Refs**: spec:2, spec:3, spec:7, spec:8
**ADR Refs**: ADR-001 (Shared Entity Extraction Library; eval-first model assignment), ADR-003 (Film Elements; model improvements reduce scaffolding)
**Depends On**: Story 035, Story 204

## Goal

Complete the Google `gemini-3.6-flash` and `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` model-slot refresh, then use the source-backed mismatches it exposed to audit CineForge's maintained tests and evals repository-wide. Repair false-green test selection, source-wrong goldens, exploitable scorers, leaked or unobservable visual targets, stale registry contracts, and material corpus blind spots without weakening valid quality gates. Re-establish which historical scores remain decision-grade before making further model-default decisions.

## Eval Ladder Context

- **Root / parent need**: `spec:8` requires evidence-backed model-slot decisions; C2 tests whether QA can shrink; C3 tests whether one model can replace tiered routing; C5 tests whether role/modality specialization can shrink.
- **Parent evals**: maintained Promptfoo tasks for `config-detection`, `scene-extraction`, `scene-enrichment`, `entity-discovery`, `script-bible`, `qa-pass`, and `video-understanding`. The latter six were most recently used for Gemini 3.5 Flash in Story 204; config detection is included because Gemini Flash currently owns that production slot.
- **Measured trigger**: live discovery on 2026-07-21 confirmed both exact API IDs are callable. `gemini-3.6-flash` is untested; `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` was falsely labeled tested because fuzzy registry matching confused it with `gemini-3.5-flash`.
- **Child eval / baseline**: add both models to the existing task configs, run only those providers with no cache, save raw evidence, and compare quality, latency, and cost with current registry leaders and runtime defaults.
- **Expanded measured trigger**: the completed model refresh found that Gemini's QA "failures" were correct rejections of a source-wrong fixture and that symbolic video targets described motion, objects, and audio absent from the rendered media. A wider read-only audit then proved that entity discovery, relationship discovery, scene extraction, and video understanding can award `1.0` to clearly invalid outputs; `make test-unit` omits 102 tests under `tests/unit`; and the maintained golden checklist certifies only 10 of 12 textual goldens while falsely marking the bad QA fixture clean.
- **Expanded child campaign**: first restore test and fixture honesty; then adversarially harden scorers; then reverify every default-driving golden/manifest with context-isolated source-first review; finally rescore cached outputs and run only bounded fresh confirmations on repaired decision surfaces.

## Out of Scope

- Changing production defaults without a clear maintained quality, latency, and cost win plus explicit user approval.
- Retuning prompts, scorers, or hand-curated goldens to help a preferred model or make a historical score pass.
- Treating schema validity, judge agreement, or a model vendor claim as a substitute for source-backed semantic review.
- Running broad paid provider matrices before the repaired harness passes its adversarial and independent-verification gates.
- Rewriting every mocked structural test when it truthfully covers plumbing; the campaign targets false confidence and default-driving decisions first.
- Production UI or runtime architecture unrelated to a source-backed test/eval defect.
- Committing or pushing changes.

## Approach Evaluation

- **Simplification baseline**: keep the smallest set of truth surfaces that can falsify bad model behavior. Remove duplicate or circular checks, but do not collapse independent source review, deterministic contracts, and semantic judging into one mechanism.
- **AI-only**: fresh context-isolated source review can catch semantic errors that structural validators miss, but it is not durable enough by itself. Each certifying verifier reads the source before the candidate golden. Some semantic snippets entered the orchestration context during later remediation; those observations did not replace the independent final verifier.
- **Hybrid (selected)**: independent AI review establishes source truth;
  deterministic source fingerprints, invariants, and adversarial mutation tests
  make that truth reproducible; the pinned rubric judge measures residual
  semantic quality, with cross-provider independence only for non-Anthropic
  subjects and separate corroboration required otherwise; cached rescoring and
  bounded live calls establish decision impact.
- **Pure code**: necessary for collection honesty, schema/path/provenance checks, exact and fuzzy matching safety, precision/recall accounting, mutation tests, and registry consistency. Pure code alone already certified the bad QA golden, so it cannot be the semantic authority.
- **Repo constraints / ADRs**: ADR-001 requires eval-first model assignment. ADR-003 allows model improvements to remove scaffolding only after measured evidence. C2, C3, and C5 remain `hold` until their detectors pass.
- **Existing patterns to reuse**: Stories 204 and 207, the golden verification/reset workflow, `golden-create`, `improve-eval`, source-backed PDF regression tests, paired false/true-positive validator tests, fixture provenance in `tests/fixtures/ingest_inputs/SOURCES.md`, existing Promptfoo tasks, and `docs/evals/registry.yaml`.
- **Eval**: repair evidence in dependency order: harness selection -> source/golden truth -> scorer/rubric discrimination -> corpus breadth -> cached rescoring -> bounded fresh calls. A score produced before its dependencies are green is diagnostic only, not adoption evidence.

## Final Audit Recommendation

The user-approved `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` configuration remains in place for
`script_bible_v1` provisionally, but the prior one-corpus score is not current
adoption proof. No repaired two-corpus, exact-runtime row is decision-grade;
require a fresh runtime-matched run before calling the default proven or
changing it. All other configured defaults likewise remain provisional. Gemini
3.6 Flash showed no uncontaminated slot win and should not be adopted from this
evidence.

The QA and frame-packet failures correctly triggered the wider campaign. Their
source, modality, and scorer defects are repaired, but buying new model/judge
evidence before the provisional contract bundle is committed would only create
another hard-to-identify dirty-worktree result. Historical rows are therefore
retained and explicitly excluded from default and methodology decisions.

Official GA contract recorded for both exact model IDs: text/image/video/audio/PDF input, text output, `1,048,576` input tokens, `65,536` output tokens, and minimal/low/medium/high thinking. Standard paid prices are `$1.50` input / `$7.50` output per million tokens for 3.6 Flash and `$0.30` / `$2.50` for 3.5 Flash-Lite. Promptfoo `0.121.1` did not yet carry these prices, so the task configs and local estimator use explicit official rates.

Relationship discovery remains a separate documented limitation: its repaired
full-screenplay task is a source-grounded capability detector, while the runtime
receives only entity names, forces 3-5 edges, and has no screenplay evidence.
It cannot drive `entity_graph_v1` selection until a runtime-shaped semantic eval
exists.

## Pre-Repair Baseline Defects

- `make test-unit` selected by `-m unit`, while seven modules physically under `tests/unit` lacked the marker. That silently omitted 102 tests, including all 30 entity-discovery module tests and all 14 character-naming regressions.
- The full test command collected three utility scripts under `tests/fixtures/round_trip/`; one errored on a nonexistent fixture and two printed failures or wrote repository outputs without failing pytest.
- `qa-good-scene.json` placed the AirTag on Rose's phone and omitted the three-thug gunfight. The source places it in her purse and continues through that gunfight. The golden nevertheless required a clean pass, and the scorer ignored its warning and required-summary constraints.
- Adversarial probes returned `1.0` for entity discovery with empty-string entities, relationship discovery with invented relationship types/evidence, scene extraction with every per-scene fact fabricated, and video understanding with every allowed tag plus hallucinated evidence.
- The entity-discovery acceptance test seeded its candidate artifact directly from the golden and measured recall only, making the live verification self-fulfilling.
- Eight of 20 video frame packets contained five byte-identical images. Several targets required cuts, drift, or orbit; rendered audio was mostly a synthetic sine tone while targets named instruments, rain, alarms, muzak, or heartbeat monitoring. The provider submitted five JPEGs plus target-adjacent title/tags/transcript/authored description, not the MP4 or audio.
- The maintained golden checklist certified ten textual fixtures and omitted the entity-discovery and script-bible goldens. Its all-clean state was contradicted by the QA source. The coverage matrix acknowledged one screenplay for the full-extraction family, no multi-screenplay continuity case, no edge-case screenplay, and one normalization source.
- `real-ai-previz-runtime` declared 13 cases while its fixture contained 15. Three historical full-script-throughput rows pointed to ignored run artifacts that no longer exist; those rows now carry explicit provenance classifications.
