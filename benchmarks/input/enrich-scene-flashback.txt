A small sailboat floats on calm water near a rocky coastline. A golden afternoon sun paints everything warm.

YOUNG MARINER (8, scrawny, gap-toothed) sits on the bow, legs dangling. DAD (late 30s, wiry, sun-weathered) crouches beside him with a fishing rod.

DAD
Now den, b'y, ya gotta feel the line. Don't watch it — feel it.

Young Mariner nods, tongue poking out in concentration. He casts — the line arcs beautifully and drops right into a patch of kelp.

DAD
Ha! Perfect cast, right inta the kitchen. That's where the big ones hide.

YOUNG MARINER
Dad, what's a hero?

Dad pauses, looks out at the water, then back at his son.

DAD
A hero's someone who does the right thing even when it's hard. Even when nobody's watchin'. Especially then.

YOUNG MARINER
Is that what you are?

DAD
(long pause)
I try to be, b'y. Every day I try.

He ruffles the boy's hair. Young Mariner beams. The sun catches them both in silhouette — a perfect father-son moment.

The line jerks. Young Mariner's eyes go wide.

YOUNG MARINER
Dad! Dad! I got one!

DAD
Well haul 'er in, me son! Haul 'er in!

They laugh together, wrestling with the rod as the boat rocks gently.
